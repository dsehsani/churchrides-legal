Safety

  • In an emergency, call 911.
  • Report a ride, person, announcement or group from its ••• menu, or by pressing and holding it. We review every report within 24 hours.
  • Block someone from the same menus. Neither of you will see the other's rides, and you won't be placed in the same car. They aren't told. Unblock anytime in Settings → Blocked people.

Common questions

How do I find my church?

Search for it during sign-up, or later in the app. Subscribe to see its events and the rides to them. If it isn't listed yet, you can register it yourself.

Who can see my phone number?

Only your driver, or riders you've approved, for that ride. Choose "Nobody" in Settings → Phone number to hide it completely.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check Settings → Notifications in the app, and make sure notifications for ChurchRides are allowed in your iPhone's Settings app.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your profile, rides and settings, as described in our Privacy Policy.
