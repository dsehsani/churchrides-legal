ChurchRides

Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what it takes to get people to church together: your name, contact details, profile, car (if you drive), the groups you join and the rides you offer or take.
  • Other members see only what they need for a ride. Your phone number goes only to your driver or riders you've approved, and you can hide it entirely.
  • We don't track your location, we don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we never sell your information.
  • You can delete your account from Settings at any time, and your information is deleted with it.
  • ChurchRides is for people 18 and older.

1. Who we are

ChurchRides ("ChurchRides", "we", "us") is an app that helps members of churches and fellowships offer and find rides to services and events. ChurchRides was founded by Darius Ehsani, Enoch Shen, Dorian Chiu and Jayden Betts (lead designer).

This policy explains what information we collect when you use the ChurchRides app and related services, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at [privacy email].

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. Your email address, used to sign you in. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us your name and email, or a private relay address if you choose to hide yours.
  • Profile. Your first and last name, phone number, age, gender, and a profile photo if you add one.
  • Driver details. If you offer rides: your car's make, model, color, year, license plate and number of seats.
  • Churches and fellowships. The groups you subscribe to. If you register a group, the details you enter for it: its name, type, denomination, address and map location, description, website, contact email and phone number, languages, and logo.
  • Rides. Rides you offer or ask to join, including times, destinations, the number of seats, and any notes you add.
  • Ride-day updates and announcements. Quick updates you send on the day of a ride (such as "On my way"), and announcements you post if you run a group.
  • Safety information. Reports you submit (the reason, any details you add, and a copy of the content you reported), the people you block, and when you agreed to our Community Guidelines.
  • Settings. Your notification choices and who can see your phone number.
  • Messages to us. Anything you send us, for example when you email for support.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token. A device identifier from Apple that lets us send notifications to your phone, if you allow them.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records basic request information, such as IP address, time, and the type of request, to keep the service secure and working. These logs are kept for a limited time.

What we don't collect

  • Your device's location. When you search for a church or enter an address, we look up that address, but the app never tracks where you are.
  • Your contacts, browsing activity, or activity in other apps.
  • Information for advertising. ChurchRides has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Information that stays on your device

Some features work only on your phone and don't send anything to us: your appearance setting, your preferred maps app, and, if you turn on "Add rides to Calendar", the calendar events the app creates for your rides. To keep those events up to date, iOS asks you to allow the app to access your calendar. The app changes only the events it created, and your calendar is never uploaded to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To run ChurchRides: to create your account, show you your groups' events, match drivers and riders, manage seat requests, and let the people on a ride coordinate.
  • To notify you: about seat requests, approvals, cancellations, ride-day updates and announcements, according to your notification settings.
  • To keep the community safe: to review reports, enforce blocks and our Community Guidelines, filter offensive language, and prevent abuse.
  • To support you: to answer your questions and fix problems.
  • To protect the service and meet legal obligations: to secure accounts, investigate misuse, and comply with the law.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. What other members can see

ChurchRides shares information between members only as far as a ride needs it.

Your car's year and license plate Riders you've approved for your ride, so they can find your car

Your email, age, gender, reports and blocks Not shown to other members. People you block aren't told.

If you block someone, neither of you can see the other's rides or profile, and you won't be placed in the same car.

5. Service providers we use

We use a small number of companies to run ChurchRides. They process your information only on our behalf and under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in, file storage (such as profile photos and group logos) and server functions, on servers in the United States.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, and delivery of push notifications.
  • Resend: sends our emails, such as sign-in links and safety notifications to our team.

When you tap Directions, the app opens Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze with the ride's destination. From then on, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Other disclosures

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that the law requires it, or that it's needed to protect someone's safety, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to protect the rights of ChurchRides or its members. If ChurchRides is ever reorganized, merged or acquired, your information may be transferred as part of that, under a privacy policy at least as protective as this one.

6. How long we keep information

  • We keep your account information for as long as your account is open.
  • Ride-day updates are deleted automatically one day after the ride.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account):
    • We delete your profile, photo, car details, the rides you drive (riders booked on them are told the ride is cancelled), your seats on other rides, your notifications, your blocks and your settings.
    • If you own a group, ownership passes to one of its admins. If it has no other admin, the group is deleted, and the app tells you before you confirm.
    • Announcements and events you posted in a group stay with that group, but are no longer linked to you.
    • If you used Sign in with Apple, we revoke ChurchRides' access to your Apple ID.
  • Reports are kept after an account is deleted so we can keep the community safe. If you made a report, it's no longer linked to your account.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups and logs may remain for a short time until they're replaced.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your information at any time in Profile.
  • Control notifications in Settings, or turn them off in iOS Settings.
  • Choose who sees your phone number in Settings → Privacy.
  • Block or report anyone from the ride screens.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete Account.
  • Ask us to give you a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing [privacy email]. We'll respond within the time the law requires, and we may need to confirm it's you first.

Depending on where you live, including California and other US states, you may have additional rights: to know what personal information we hold about you, to access, correct or delete it, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising, and we don't use sensitive information to infer things about you. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address above.

8. Age requirement

ChurchRides is only for people 18 and older, and isn't directed to children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we'll delete it. If you believe a minor is using ChurchRides, please contact us.

9. How we protect your information

We use industry-standard safeguards:

  • All connections are encrypted.
  • Database rules make sure each person can read only what this policy describes.
  • Profile photos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links.
  • Sensitive operations, like revealing a phone number, run on our servers where they can be checked.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

10. Where your information is stored

ChurchRides is operated from the United States, and your information is stored and processed there. If you use ChurchRides from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ChurchRides changes. When we do, we'll change the effective date above. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app before they take effect.
